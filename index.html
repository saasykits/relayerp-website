Questions and answers

What is an ERP, and do I need one?

An ERP keeps your stock, buying, selling, cash and production in one system. If your numbers are scattered across spreadsheets, you probably need one.

Is RelayERP right for a small business?

Yes. The Standard plan covers up to 10 members. Larger teams can ask us for custom limits.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $9.99 a month. AI assistant use runs on credits you buy. You can try it free for 7 days.

How long does it take to set up?

Create your organization and invite your team by email. We review each new organization before first use and help you get set up.

Is my data safe?

We use encrypted connections, access controls and backups. Your records belong to your organization.

Is it cloud-based, and can I get my data out?

Yes, we host it, so you have no servers to run. You can export your lists, statements and documents at any time.

How does the AI assistant use our data?

It only sees what the person asking is allowed to see, and it creates nothing until someone accepts it. Requests and the relevant records are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response.
